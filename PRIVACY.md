# Pet Island — Privacy Policy

Updated 17 October 2026. Contact: **bortone52@yandex.ru**.

## Local island data

Pet names, colors, settings, care, wardrobe, enclosure layout, walks, postcards and arcade progress are stored on your device. The app and its widget/Live Activity extension share the necessary data through an App Group. There is no developer-hosted account, backend, advertising SDK or analytics/tracking SDK. No location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising identifier is requested.

## Optional support payments

Apple processes voluntary, one-time tips through StoreKit and the App Store. The app verifies transactions on the device and acknowledges completed tips. Tips do not grant coins, subscriptions or additional game features. Pet Island does not receive card details or send receipts or transaction identifiers to a developer server. Apple handles and may retain purchase records under its own policies: [App Store privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/).

## Export, sharing and backups

An island backup is exported only at your request. You choose its destination in the system interface. It can contain pet names and progress; anyone with access to the file can read them. Import replaces local island data after confirmation. Postcards are shared only when you choose to share them. Your chosen storage/sharing service and iOS device backups operate under their own policies. Manual export is not automatic cross-device synchronization.

## Support correspondence

If you contact **bortone52@yandex.ru**, your email address, message and any attachments are used to answer your request. Include only information needed to resolve it. The email provider handles correspondence under its own policy. Ask for deletion by emailing the same address. Do not send passwords or card information.

## Removing data

You can delete individual pets in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data; copies in device backups or exported files must be removed separately. Removing Pet Island does not remove Apple's purchase records. There is no app account to close.

## Changes

The app includes this policy offline under Settings → Help and privacy. The public copy must be hosted at the Privacy Policy URL supplied in App Store Connect. Adding advertising, analytics, server-side processing or other collection requires updating this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before release.
