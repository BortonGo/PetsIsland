Pet Island

Privacy policy

Updated 17 October 2026

Pet Island keeps your island on your device. The app has no developer-hosted account or backend, advertising, analytics SDK or tracking SDK.

Data on your device

Pet names, colors, settings, care, wardrobe, furniture, friendship, walks, postcards and game progress are stored locally. The app, widget and Live Activity share the data they need through an App Group, a shared store on your device. The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or an advertising identifier.

Optional payments

Apple processes one-time tips through StoreKit and the App Store. Pet Island verifies transactions on your device. Tips grant no coins, subscriptions or game features. Neither the app nor the author receives card details. The app does not send receipts or purchase identifiers to a developer server. Apple processes and may retain payment information under its App Store privacy policy.

Backups and sharing

An island backup is exported only when you request it. It may contain pet names and progress; anyone with access to the file can read them. You choose where to save it and whom to send it to. Import replaces the current island after confirmation and saves the previous version for recovery. Postcards are shared only when you choose. iOS device backups and selected storage or sharing services follow their own policies. There is no automatic cross-device sync.

Support messages

If you write to bortone52@yandex.ru, your address, message and attachments are used to answer your request. Correspondence is stored in the support mailbox and processed by the email provider under its rules. To request deletion of correspondence, write to the same address. Send only what is needed, without passwords or payment details.

Deleting data

You can delete individual pets in the app. Deleting Pet Island removes its local data; exported files, device backups and shared postcards must be removed separately from the services you chose. Offloading an app in iOS while keeping its documents does not delete those documents. Deleting Pet Island does not delete Apple’s purchase records. There is no app account to close.

This website

These pages contain no forms, cookies, analytics scripts or external fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes network requests to serve the pages and may retain IP addresses and request details in technical logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visits to Apple’s websites and sending email are also subject to the respective services’ policies.

Policy changes

The main policy is available offline in the app under Settings → Help and privacy. If data handling changes, this page and the app’s privacy disclosures must be updated before releasing a new version.

For privacy questions: bortone52@yandex.ru.
