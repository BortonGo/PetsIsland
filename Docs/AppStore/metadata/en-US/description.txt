A little island for your pixel pets. Choose a cat, corgi, fox or another companion, give them a name and make them a cozy home.

Life on the island
Choose a landscape, place furniture and watch your pets wander, rest and meet each other. Care for them and pick accessories to suit their mood.

Walks and discoveries
Send your pets to explore different places. Check in on their walks, collect discoveries, read little stories and keep postcards as souvenirs.

Play together
Four arcade games for a short break: platform hopping, flying, obstacle runs and colorful bubbles. Earn in-game coins for food, accessories and enclosure furniture.

A companion close by
Bring a pet to your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island on a compatible iPhone. Choose the activity's background and add an enclosure widget to your Home Screen.

No sign-up, ads or tracking. Progress stays on your device. You can manually export and import a backup of your island; there is no automatic sync between devices.

Supporting the author is an optional one-time tip through the App Store. Tips do not unlock pets, coins or extra features. There are no subscriptions.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Dynamic Island requires a supported iPhone. iOS controls widget and Live Activity refresh timing; pets rest on Always-On Display.
